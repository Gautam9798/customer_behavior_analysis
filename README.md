# 👨🏻‍💻 Customer Shopping Behavior Analysis

An end-to-end data analytics project analyzing **3,900 customer purchases** to uncover shopping patterns, customer segments, product performance, and subscription behavior.

## 📌 Project Overview

The project follows a complete analytics workflow:

- 🐍 **Python** – Data cleaning, preprocessing & EDA
- 🗄️ **PostgreSQL / SQL** – Business analysis & customer segmentation
- 📊 **Power BI** – Interactive dashboard & visualization
- 💡 **Business Insights** – Recommendations based on analysis

## 🎯 Key Business Questions

- Which customer segments generate the most revenue?
- How does discount usage affect purchasing behavior?
- Which products have the highest ratings?
- How do subscribers compare with non-subscribers?
- Which products are most dependent on discounts?
- What are the most purchased products in each category?
- How does revenue vary across age groups?

## 📊 Key Insights

- **3,116** customers were classified as Loyal.
- **701** customers were classified as Returning.
- **83** customers were classified as New.
- Express shipping had an average purchase amount of **$60.48** compared with **$58.46** for Standard shipping.
- **Gloves** had the highest average product rating at **3.86**.
- **Hat** had the highest percentage of discounted purchases.

## 📈 Power BI Dashboard

The interactive dashboard includes:

- Total Customers
- Average Purchase Amount
- Average Rating
- Revenue & Sales by Category
- Revenue & Sales by Age Group
- Subscription Status
- Gender, Category & Shipping filters
